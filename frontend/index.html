About CyberCore

AI-Powered Cyber Fraud Intelligence

CyberCore is a unified digital-forensics workspace designed to help investigators analyze fragmented cyber-fraud evidence from multiple sources.

01 Multi-source evidence
02 Entity correlation
03 Risk intelligence

How CyberCore Works

UPLOAD → PARSE → CORRELATE → RISK → REPORT

Evidence is normalized, entities are extracted, relationships are identified and suspicious patterns are highlighted for investigation.

Forensic Integrity

Every uploaded artifact can be fingerprinted using SHA-256 before processing. This helps preserve evidence integrity and allows investigators to verify whether the artifact has changed.
